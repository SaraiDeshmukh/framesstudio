2. What the platform does

The platform shows eyeglass frames and lets you see them on your face using your device’s camera or a photo you choose. It offers:

  • Demo frames: a small set of sample frames made for demonstration. They are not real products and are not for sale.
  • Participating shops’ frames: frames listed by independent optical shops (“partner shops”), with the prices, GST information and details they provide.
  • A shortlist to compare frames, and an optional pupil distance (PD) field to show frames at their real size.

The platform does not sell frames, take orders or accept payments.

3. No account needed

You can browse, use the demo and try frames on without creating an account or giving any personal details. Accounts exist only for partner shops and the platform administrator.

4. Virtual try-on is a preview, not a fitting

The try-on shows an approximate picture of how a frame might look. It is created from a photograph of the frame and an estimate of your face’s position, and can differ from reality in size, colour, shape, depth and position. It is not:

  • a guarantee of how a frame will fit, feel or look on you;
  • an eye examination, a prescription, or advice about whether a frame suits your prescription or your eyes;
  • an exact measurement. Real-size display depends on the frame size the shop entered and the PD you enter; without them, averages are used.

Always try a frame on in the shop and follow the advice of a qualified optometrist or ophthalmologist about lenses and fitting.
