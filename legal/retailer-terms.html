1. The service

The platform lets you list your frames, with their details, prices and GST information, and lets customers try them on virtually from your shop screen or their own devices and then contact or visit you. It prepares try-on images automatically from photos you take. The platform is not a seller, agent or payment intermediary: it does not take orders or payments for your frames.

2. Eligibility, registration and approval

  • You must be a business that sells eyewear in India, and the person registering must be an adult authorised to accept these terms for it.
  • You must give accurate information in your application and keep it up to date.
  • We review each application and may approve or reject it at our discretion. If we reject it, we tell you why and you may correct and re-apply.
  • Until your shop is approved you can prepare frames, but nothing is shown to customers.

3. Your account and its security

  • Keep your password secret and don’t share your account. You’re responsible for what is done with it.
  • On shared shop screens, don’t stay signed in: use the kiosk address for customers instead.
  • Tell us at once at [support email] if you think someone else has used your account.

4. Your listings

You are responsible for your listings being accurate, complete, lawful and up to date, including the frame’s name, brand, description, size, material, photos, price, GST information and availability. Only list frames you actually offer. Archive or remove listings you no longer offer. Where a listing names a brand, you confirm the frame is genuine.

5. Prices, GST and your legal obligations

  • You set your own prices. You choose, for each frame, whether the price includes GST, has GST added, or has no GST charged, and the applicable rate, and you confirm it before the frame can be published.
  • The platform calculates the price before GST, the GST amount and the final price from your figures. This is arithmetic, not tax advice: we do not check or guarantee your tax classification, rate or compliance.
  • You are responsible for complying with all laws that apply to your business and your listings, including GST law, consumer protection law, legal metrology requirements and any rules on the sale of optical goods.
  • The price you display must be the price you honour when a customer buys, or you must tell the customer clearly before the sale.

6. Your photos: ownership and permission

  • You keep ownership of the photos and content you upload, or confirm you have permission to use them.
  • You give the platform a non-exclusive, royalty-free licence, for as long as the content is on the platform and for [30 days] after, to store, copy, process and transform your photos (for example to cut out the frame, make thumbnails, fitting data and 3D try-on models) and to display the results and your listings on the platform, for the purpose of providing the service.
  • We keep your original photos privately so try-on images can be remade; only you and the administrator can access them. Processed images of published frames are publicly viewable.
  • Upload photos of frames only. Never upload photos of customers or other people.

7. The try-on is a preview

Try-on images are estimates made from a photo and can differ from the real frame. You must not present the try-on as a guarantee of fit, comfort, appearance or prescription suitability, or as an eye examination. Customers are told to try frames in person.

8. Content you must not upload

Do not upload or list anything that is unlawful, false or misleading, counterfeit, infringes anyone’s trademark, copyright or other rights, contains people’s personal data or photos, is obscene, abusive or harmful, or contains malicious code. Do not try to access other shops’ data or interfere with the platform.

9. Customers and sales

Customers contact you directly using the details you publish. Any sale, payment, delivery, fitting, return, refund or warranty is between you and the customer, and you are responsible for your obligations to them under law. Handle customer complaints about your frames promptly and fairly.

10. Data, security and confidentiality

  • Your private details (such as owner name, phone and GSTIN), drafts, original photos and unpublished listings are visible only to you and the platform administrator. Other shops cannot see or change your private data or unpublished inventory, and you cannot see theirs; this is enforced by the platform’s server rules.
  • The platform does not collect customers’ personal data through the try-on, so no customer data is shared with you through it. Any personal data customers give you directly is yours to handle lawfully.
  • How we handle your data is described in the privacy policy.

  • When the agreement ends, your listings are removed and your data is handled as described in the privacy policy.
