Privacy policy

Version 1.0 · Last updated 12 October 2026

In short

  • Customers: your camera video and any photo you choose for try-on are processed in your browser and are never uploaded, saved, shared or used for training by this site. You don’t need an account. There are no analytics or advertising trackers. We only count how many times each frame is tried on (a number per shop, per frame and per day), with nothing about you attached.
  • Partner shops: we store your account (email address), your shop profile, your frames with their photos, prices and GST details, and a record of the terms you accepted.
  • We use Google Firebase (accounts, database, file storage) and GitHub Pages (web hosting). We don’t sell personal data.

2. Customers and visitors

You can use the whole customer side of the site (the demo, shops’ catalogues, frame pages, try-on, the shortlist and the PD field) without an account and without giving us your name, phone number or email address. We do not use cookies or similar technologies for analytics or advertising, and the site contains no analytics, advertising or social-media tracking scripts. When a frame is shown on your face (camera or photo), the site adds one to a count for that frame and its shop, for that day. The count is just a number: no name, account, device identifier, IP address, photo or cookie is stored with it. Shops see their own counts and the administrator sees all shops’ counts.

3. Camera and photos (try-on)

  • Where it happens. Face tracking uses Google’s MediaPipe Face Landmarker, a library that runs inside your browser. The library and its face model are downloaded to your device the first time you use try-on; your images are not sent to Google or anyone else.
  • Nothing is uploaded or kept. The site does not upload, transmit, record, save or share your camera video, any photo you choose, or measurements or landmarks derived from your face. The site has no feature to take, download or share pictures of you, and our storage has no place where such pictures could be saved.
  • In memory only, while you use it. While you try frames on, your browser holds the current camera image or your chosen photo in memory so it can be shown and analysed. When you replace the photo or leave the try-on page, and after 20 minutes without use, the site stops the camera and clears the images it was holding. The camera also stops whenever the tab is hidden or closed, and closing the tab discards everything. Your browser or operating system may keep freed memory for a short time; the site keeps no copy.
  • Camera permission. Your browser asks before the camera is used. You can refuse and use a photo instead, or withdraw the permission at any time in your browser settings.
  • Not used for training. Your images are never used to train or improve any model.

5. Reporting a listing

If you report a listing, we store the reason you choose, any note you write, the listing and shop concerned, the time, and a random technical identifier. To limit reports to one per minute per device, your browser is given an anonymous Firebase account (a random id with no name, email or phone number attached). Please don’t include personal details in the note. Only the platform administrator can read reports.

6. Technical data seen by service providers

Like any website, when your browser loads pages and files, the services that deliver them receive technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the time of the request. These services are: GitHub Pages (our web host), Google Firebase (the shops’ catalogue data and frame pictures), jsDelivr (a content delivery network for the try-on software libraries), Google Cloud Storage (the face-tracking model file) and Google Fonts (fonts). They process this data under their own privacy policies, for example to deliver the files and keep their services secure. We do not receive these access logs ourselves except in the aggregated usage figures our providers show us (such as the number of database reads). 12. Security

Access to stored data is enforced on the server by Firebase security rules: shops can only read and change their own records and files, customers can only read published listings, and only an administrator account created directly in the Firebase console can perform administrative actions. Connections use HTTPS, and Google encrypts stored data. Shared shop screens sign out automatically. If a personal data breach occurs, we will inform affected people and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.
