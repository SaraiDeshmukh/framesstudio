Add frames

Adding and editing frames is for shop staff.

Temple photo

Re-process the saved original photo with the current settings, or take/upload a better photo of the same frame. Tags and size stay as they are.

Photograph one temple (arm) from the side: open the frame, lay it on its side or hold it so the temple is flat and faces the camera straight-on, hinge on the left. The same background tips apply. This replaces the generated temple in the 3D try-on.

Frame photo

Recommended. Photograph the glasses straight on, filling most of the picture. Lying on a table or on a display stand is fine; no special background needed. The frame, lens openings and fitting points are found automatically and you check them before saving.

First photograph the empty surface alone, then place the frame and photograph again without moving the camera. The saved surface photo carries over for the next frame too, so you only need to retake it if the camera moves.

Sends the photo to remove.bg for background removal. It needs an internet connection and counts against your remove.bg usage.

    How the camera and photos are used

    Face tracking runs in this browser. Camera video and photos stay on this device: they are not recorded, saved or sent anywhere, and the camera turns off when you leave the try-on page. Your shortlist and PD are kept only until you leave the screen for a while. The shop uses Google Analytics to count how the site is used, for example how often frames are tried on; no photos, video or face data are included.
